# Privacy Policy for FloraVision AI

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026

FloraVision AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
* **Camera & Photo Data:** FloraVision AI requests access to your device camera and photo library solely to allow you to capture or select images of plants, trees, and flowers for botanical identification.
* **Image Transmission:** Captured images are transmitted securely over HTTPS encryption to our AI processing engines (Google Gemini API) to extract botanical features and generate remedy information. Images are processed ephemerally and are not permanently stored on external servers or sold to third parties.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use image data exclusively to:
* Identify plant species and health attributes.
* Provide accurate herbal medicine, preparation methods, and safety guidance.

## 3. Data Storage & Local History
* All scan history and saved plant records are stored locally on your device storage (`localStorage`).
* You can clear your entire scan history at any time through the app's History tab.

## 4. Permissions Required
* `CAMERA`: Used to snap photos of plants in real-time.
* `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`: Used to pick existing plant photos from your gallery.

## 5. Medical Disclaimer
FloraVision AI provides botanical and traditional herbal information for educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

For privacy questions regarding FloraVision AI, contact: support@floravision.app
